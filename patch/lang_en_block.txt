option.DOF_FOCAL_LENGTH.comment=Focal length of the virtual lens in metres. Smaller values increase the blur of everything behind the focus point.

option.DOF_MAX_BLUR_RADIUS.comment=Hard limit of the blur radius, in units of 15 pixels at 1080p. Default is 1.0.
